# Voxel runtime cutover

`project.godot` now launches `scenes/main/VoxelMain.tscn`. The previous `scenes/main/Main.tscn` remains available as the legacy 2D regression fixture because the existing integration suite directly instantiates it and verifies its procedural restart contract; it is no longer the project entry scene.

`VoxelMain` generates the deterministic origin Prague model with `ProceduralCityGenerator`, converts it with `SemanticVoxelizer`, and merges it into `VoxelWorldData`. `VoxelChunkStreamController` builds the active base chunk without roof material and with collision. `VoxelBuildingRoofRuntime` groups roof cells by their existing stable building source ID, creates one merged roof renderer per building, and registers each renderer with `VoxelRoofOcclusionController3D` and `VoxelStructuralDamageService`.

The active actor scenes are `VoxelPlayer3D.tscn`, `VoxelZombie3D.tscn`, and `VoxelSurvivor3D.tscn`. The player uses two existing `WeaponData` resources, pooled 3D projectiles, `HealthComponent`, `InputRouter`, and the production HUD. Eight zombies are sampled deterministically from generated spawn regions, acquire only player/survivor attackable targets, navigate the voxel grid, and apply eight contact damage on a 0.6-second cadence. Four survivors use their existing persistent `SurvivorData`, inventory, pistol, utility decisions, semantic job board, and categorized settlement storage.

Generated doors, scavenge points, loot furniture, and exterior props are instantiated by `VoxelSemanticRuntime`. Structural cell edits update `VoxelWorldData`, sparse overrides, the base renderer, and the owning building roof renderer. Pause, death, restart, mobile controls, HUD counts, interaction prompts, camera follow/zoom, stable seed reuse, and autoload reset flow are wired in `VoxelMain`.

`VoxelMain` now follows the player's chunk coordinate through a retained 3-by-3 visual working set. `VoxelChunkGenerationQueue` prepares at most two deterministic city-plus-voxel payloads concurrently from a main-thread snapshot of persistent door/prop state. The main thread integrates at most one ready renderer per frame, and a not-ready payload remains pending rather than disappearing from the stream request. Chunks on the expired edge are unloaded while the six overlapping chunks remain intact.

Only the active chunk has collision during ordinary movement. Approaching a loaded boundary promotes the adjacent collision mesh and activates that chunk's independently hideable roofs and semantic interaction areas before entry; visual-only prefetch chunks do not pay that gameplay-node cost. A center transition commits only after the destination renderer is loaded, retains cross-boundary navigation data, and performs no generation or mesh build in the transition call. Local zombies are replaced from the entered chunk's spawn regions while the same four survivor nodes, IDs, inventories, and utility data are rebound to the retained navigation and semantic services.

The production `SurvivorInspector` accepts both legacy 2D and voxel 3D survivor nodes. Voxel selection projects the right-click camera ray onto the actor plane and selects within 1.6 world units. `VoxelWorldDropRuntime3D` mirrors `WorldState.drop_registered`, creates a colored collectible cargo box, transfers its exact `Inventory`, and removes the persistent drop only when empty. `VoxelCombatEffects3D` consumes voxel damage, death, and explosion signals for hit flashes, expanding bursts, and a capped 96-decal blood pool. `DebugOverlay` reports voxel chunk queues, merged voxel/face counts, projectile pool usage, survivor utility decision rates, and semantic job reservations while retaining its legacy manager path.

`voxel_main_smoke.gd` verifies the production startup path, bounded 3-by-3 prefetch, drained worker queue, shared boundary navigation, center-plus-boundary collision promotion, no-build/no-wait transition commits, retained overlap, entering-edge-only rebuild counts, generated semantic population, per-building roof renderers, four survivors, bounded zombies, HUD state, mobile zoom routing, zombie contact damage, post-transition weapon/projectile combat, semantic door and loot interaction, explosive-only structural damage, survivor job rebinding/harvesting, faction targeting, generated roof hide/restore, survivor retention, destroyed-voxel persistence, 3D survivor inspection, exact world-drop collection, and bounded combat-effect events. `VoxelChunkGenerationQueueTest.tscn` independently verifies the two-thread cap, cancellation outside the retained set, deterministic equivalence, off-thread voxel payloads, and empty consumed cache. The D3D12 capture argument `--capture-voxel-main=<absolute png path>` renders the active project entry after 90 frames for visual QA; `--capture-voxel-main-chunk=x,z` moves that capture to a streamed chunk, `--capture-voxel-main-mobile` forces the touch controls visible, `--capture-voxel-main-inspector` opens the selected-survivor panel, `--capture-voxel-main-drop` adds a capture-only death cargo box, and `--capture-voxel-main-effects` stages a blood decal plus hit/explosion bursts.
